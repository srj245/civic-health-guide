# Civic Health Hub

Build CivicCare, an all-in-one civic health companion that helps people understand how government decisions and breaking news affect their health—and shows them what they can do about it.

Healthcare policies, public health news, and government updates are often confusing, overwhelming, and written in language most people don’t understand. CivicCare changes that. It translates complex health-related laws and news into plain English, explains who is affected and why it matters, and connects users to real healthcare resources in their community.

With CivicCare, users can:

Understand healthcare policies without needing a law degree

Get simple explanations of health-related news and legislation

See how government decisions impact students, families, seniors, and workers

Find nearby clinics, mental health services, and low-cost care options

Take informed action instead of feeling lost or excluded

CivicCare is nonpartisan, accessible, and built for everyday people—especially students and first-time voters—who want to stay informed without being overwhelmed. By connecting civic information to real-world health support, CivicCare turns awareness into impact and confusion into clarity.
